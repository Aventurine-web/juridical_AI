# Partnerships — Vorker Compliance Copilot

Distribution and trust first — both matter for a compliance product.

## Distribution partners (reach the SME)
- **Accounting software — Fortnox, Visma, Bokio:** they own the daily SME relationship.
  Marketplace app / embedded copilot. Highest-leverage channel.
- **Banks (SEB, Handelsbanken, Nordea SME):** company onboarding is a trigger moment;
  offer the copilot in their SME portal.
- **verksamt.se & Almi:** the official starting point for new businesses; referral / integration.

## Trust & content partners (credibility)
- **Redovisnings- & revisionsbyråer:** B2B2C — they advise clients faster with an audit trail;
  also a source of domain feedback to keep the agent accurate.
- **Law firms / jurister:** "escalate to a human" handoff for complex cases (and a referral loop).

## Source / platform partners
- **Google (ADK + Gemini):** the stack; lean into the grounded-agent story.
- **Skatteverket / Bolagsverket open data & APIs:** deeper, fresher grounding over time.

## Integration partners (context-aware advice)
- Accounting data (Fortnox/Visma) → the copilot reasons with the company's real numbers.
- Contract/e-sign tools (Scrive, Oneflow) → analyse agreements where they're signed.

## For each partner, capture
| Partner | They give us | They get | First step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fortnox/Visma | SME distribution | A compliance feature, stickier product | Marketplace listing / pilot |
| Redovisningsbyrå | Trust + feedback | Faster client advice, audit trail | 2–3 pilot firms |
| Bank SME portal | Trigger-moment reach | Differentiated onboarding | Referral pilot |
